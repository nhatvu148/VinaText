LGPLv3 compliance
-----------------

These obligations are binding on every binary VinaText ships. See
doc/QT-PORT-BRIEF.md section 4, D3.

  1. Qt MUST be linked DYNAMICALLY. Never link Qt statically - doing so can
     place the whole application under the LGPL unless relinkable object files
     are also published.
  2. Do not defeat relinking. No signing or packaging that prevents a recipient
     replacing the Qt libraries with their own build.
  3. State the exact Qt version in the About dialog, together with the words
     "Uses Qt under LGPLv3".
  4. Offer recipients the corresponding source for that exact Qt version.
  5. Publish any patches made to Qt itself. VinaText makes none.

The full LGPLv3 text is at https://www.gnu.org/licenses/lgpl-3.0.txt and MUST be
included in shipped packages. It is referenced rather than inlined here because
no VinaText binary links Qt yet - ui-qt/ is behind the VINATEXT_BUILD_QT CMake
option, which defaults OFF. Bundling the full text is a packaging task, tracked
for Phase 5/6.

Corresponding source for the Qt version in use: https://download.qt.io/archive/qt/
